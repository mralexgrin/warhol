2 · One language for the score

Each option is shown at every scale it has to survive: three list rows, a report header, and the sticky bar. The test is not which looks best alone — it is whether a column of ten stays comparable, and whether confidence makes it onto the list at all.

A · the ring, everywhere

Number is the score, sweep is confidence. Two independent quantities in one mark, no legend. The gap/strain split drops off the list and lives on the report, where there is room to state it in words.
