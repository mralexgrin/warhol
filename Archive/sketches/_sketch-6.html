2 · The header handoff

To answer directly: the tall header is not sticky — at ~250px it would eat a third of the viewport permanently. It is the arrival, and it scrolls away. A compact bar then takes over carrying back, name, ring and the two verbs, so you can decide from anywhere in the report. Scroll the box, then open a section — the bar holds and your position holds.
