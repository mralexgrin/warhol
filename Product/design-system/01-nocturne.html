Depth & light

Three layered shadows with real vertical offset. Gradients are atmospheric, not decorative: a dusk field for the hero, a forest wash for the gap card, and an ambient glow that sits behind the headline rather than on it.

Flat

Hairline only. Grouping, filter shelves.

Gradients

Dusk. Cream → peach → amber on a 168° axis. The hero card's ground, and the only place a rendered landform appears.

Forest wash. 146°, two stops. The gap card and any chart whose subject is a counted quantity.

Ambient glow. Two soft radials at low alpha, always behind type at arm's length — never a halo on a small element.

Controls

Every control is a pill. On a light card the primary action is a black pill; on ink it's amber; on a colour field it's outlined in the field's own text colour. Focus is a 2px amber ring at 3px offset, legible on all six grounds.

Night is the default — this is a black-canvas world and the reference is built that way. Day keeps the same four card colours on a warm paper ground.
