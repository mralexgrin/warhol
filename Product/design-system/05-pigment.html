Pigments

Four colours, each able to carry a whole panel. Every pigment has a deep variant for text and lines on neutral, and a soft variant for pill backgrounds — so a colour never has to be faded with opacity to stay legible.

Primary action, all body text, and the dark theme's ground. The only colour that is also a surface in both themes.

The commitment rule. A pigment owns a whole panel, a whole bar, or a whole chip — never a 3px stripe on a white card. If a colour can't justify the full surface, it doesn't belong on the screen. This is what keeps the world from collapsing into generic accent-on-neutral SaaS.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for display — a variable grotesque with enough irregularity to have a face, run at width 92 and −0.055em so headlines lock up tight. Onest for interface: quiet, large x-height, excellent tabular figures.

Shape

Radii are the loudest signal in this world: 24px on panels, full pill on every control and every bar. Nothing here is square, which is exactly what separates it from the four toolkits beside it.

Depth & Blend

Three layered shadows, each with a real vertical offset and a soft blur — never a zero-offset halo. Gradients are legitimate in this world and only this one: mesh for hero fields, duo for chart fills, veil for text over colour.

Line · no shadow

Grouping only. Filters, secondary containers.

el-1 · resting

Default panel. Cards, rows, inputs.

el-2 · raised

Hover, selected candidate, hero fields.

el-3 · floating

Menus, drawers, the brief modal.

Gradients — the three permitted forms

Mesh. Three closest-side radial stops over a neutral. Hero fields and the empty state. Never behind body text.

Duo. Two pigments on a 118° axis. Chart area fills and the score arc.

Veil. An ink gradient over a duo, so text on a colour field always clears 4.5:1 without dulling the colour.

Controls

Everything is a pill. The primary action is ink — pigments carry meaning, so spending one on a button would break the code the Scout has learned. Focus is a lilac ring at 3px offset, visible on every ground including the colour fields.

Actions

Hover lifts 2px on an exponential ease-out and deepens to el-2. That single lift is the world's whole motion vocabulary — reused for rows, cards and the drawer.

Tokens

Light is the default — this desk is worked in a lit office in the morning. Dark reuses #1C1B23 from the source palette as its ground, so the pigments sit on a colour that was always part of the system.
