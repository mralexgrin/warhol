Panel fields

Five fields. A panel takes one entirely — the hue is the label. Text on coral, sky and cream is ink; on periwinkle it's the cool off-white. Lemon never fills a panel; it's the alert tile only.

Coral · #F0655A

Score

The composite and anything ranked. The loudest field, reserved for the one question the Scout opens the app to answer.

Periwinkle · #6C63E8

Coverage

Scan health, cadence, projections. Anything that is about the machine rather than a candidate.

Sky · #C8E4F2

Where

Platform and audience distribution. The quietest field — it carries context, not verdicts.

Cream · #EFEFE7

Timing

The scan clock and anything on a schedule. Also the ground for every white control on a dark panel.

Lemon · #F5C842

Alert icon tiles and inline highlight chips. Never a panel fill, never a chart series.

Pass · #5FCB8E

Gate cleared and positive deltas. Appears as a solid delta chip, or an 18% pill tint.

Slab · #1C201D on page #C3D3C3

The shell. The sage page is visible on all four sides at all times — that margin is the design.

Fail · #F0655A

Shares coral deliberately: a refusal is a score outcome, so it lives in the score's colour.

Depth & fill

The slab casts the only large shadow; panels inside it sit flat, separated by colour rather than elevation. Hatching — not opacity — marks anything projected, pending or incomplete.

Flat

Every panel inside the slab. Colour does the separating.

el-1

Hover, and controls that leave the surface.

el-2

Menus, the mandate selector when open.

el-3 + slab

Drawers and the report overlay. The slab itself uses a wider, softer pair.

Hatch — the signature fill

Solid = happened. Hatched = projected. 48°, 2px stripe, 7px pitch, drawn in the field's own ink at 34–42%. Used on bars, capacity chips and gauge remainders.

Gradients

Heat. Coral → lemon, 126°. Score surfaces.

Cool. Periwinkle → sky. Coverage surfaces.

Series fade. Under a line, vertical, to zero.

Controls

On the slab, actions are cream pills. Inside a colour field they become translucent white so the field stays unbroken. The circular ↗ is the only way a panel opens its own detail view, and it rotates 45° on hover.
