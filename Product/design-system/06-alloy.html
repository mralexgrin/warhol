Depth & light

Pigment's three layered shadows, each with a real vertical offset — no zero-offset halos. Gradients are ambient: mesh behind a field, duo under a chart, and the veil, which exists purely so type on colour keeps its ratio.

Gradients

Mesh. Three closest-corner radials over a neutral. Behind a field, at low alpha, never behind body text.

Duo. Lilac → teal on a 118° axis. Chart area fills and the empty state.

Legible on colour

Veil. An ink gradient over a duo. Any text over a gradient sits on the veil, so it clears 4.5:1 regardless of what the gradient does underneath.

Controls & states

Every state is shown, because a design system that only specifies rest is a system that fails on hover. Hover never lowers contrast here — fields lighten rather than darken, so ink text gains ratio instead of losing it.

On the slab

Hover lifts 2px on an exponential ease-out and deepens to el-2 — the one authored motion, reused by rows, cards and the drawer. It is suppressed entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.

The rule that came out of the audit: a hovered field lightens. Darkening a pastel under dark text is the intuitive move and it costs ratio every time — the primary button is the only surface that lifts toward the text, and it starts at 16.40 so it can afford to.

Focus — one ring, six grounds
On panel On butter On teal

A 3px ring plus a 6px halo in the opposite value. The ring alone would fail on at least one field in each theme; the halo is what makes a single token work everywhere. Tab through this page to see it — focus is :focus-visible only, so pointer users never see it.

The rest of the floor
  • Status never rides on colour alone — every pill carries its word.
  • Placeholders are text: 5.19:1, not the usual near-invisible grey.
  • Errors name the problem and the recovery, and are tied by aria-describedby.
  • Charts carry a full sentence in aria-label, including the projected values.
  • Rail and header controls are 44 px targets; in-row buttons are 32 px, which is the one place this system trades size for density — flagged, not hidden.
  • prefers-reduced-motion removes the lift; prefers-contrast: more deepens secondary text and both line tokens.
  • A skip link is the first tab stop.

Toolkit 06 — ALLOY. Mosaic's slab, rail, saturated fields, hatch and circular ↗; Pigment's mesh, veil, radii and layered depth; Daylight's Epilogue / Be Vietnam Pro pairing. Best when you want Mosaic's information architecture without its hard edges, and you need the contrast to survive a procurement review. Weakest at extreme density — the 24px panels and 44px targets cost rows, so a 40-row table wants a compact variant.
