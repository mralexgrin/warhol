One hue

Nine steps of a single green carry every positive quantity in the product. Status is the only place other hues appear, and only as a tinted pill — never as a fill, a bar, or a chart series.

Hatch — the signature fill

Solid green = worked. Hatched grey = remaining. 45°, 2px stripe, 7px pitch, drawn in the page's own ink at 18%. Used on bars, the progress gauge, and any future-dated cell.

Toolkit 04 — DAYLIGHT. Built from the Donezo reference. Best when the desk is worked in a lit office all day and the product should feel calm and unhurried — one hue means nothing shouts, and hatching answers "what's left?" without a second colour. Weakest when the drop needs urgency: this world is deliberately unexcited.
