Scout — proposed changes

Ten changes drawn from the critique of v5.8, rendered in Scout’s own CSS so you can see them at real size before anything is committed. In every pair, Now is the running build and Proposed is the change. Nothing in app.js, ui.js or scout-v52.css has been touched.

Prototype fidelity · items 10–12 (mobile breakpoint, rail button names, Trends keyboard access) are out of scope by your call and are not shown here.

The drop row

3 · 7 · 13 · 14 The row carries the verb, the scale and readable labels

Four changes land on one component, so they are shown together. The ring is drawn against 100 with a tick at the bar instead of against an observed ceiling of 40. The DEMAND / MISSING / PRESSURE labels go from 9.5px to 11px. The Watch and Pass outlines darken to clear 3:1.

Adding Promote made four buttons, which is one too many. It stays three: promote · watch · pass — the three verbs Scout already names. Open the report is not a decision, it is a destination, so it moves onto the creator’s name and the row itself. Two ways to show that are below.

The Pass tray

6 Three reasons, not eight

Pass is the most-used action in the product and currently the slowest screen: eight reasons, each with its own comeback rule, all shown at once. The proposal keeps every reason available but shows the three that cover most passes, shortens each comeback rule to something readable at a glance, and leads with the reassurance instead of ending on it.

Now
Pass, with a reasonThe reason is the suppression rule and the training label. Nobody vanishes.

Nine options. Eight reversibility policies to parse to pass one name. The tray pushes the rest of the drop down about 400px.

Proposed
Pass, with a reasonNobody vanishes. Every pass carries your reason and what brings the name back.

Three reasons cover most passes, and each comeback rule is short enough to read without stopping. The other five live behind Another reason. “Suppression rule” and “training label” are engine words and are gone.
