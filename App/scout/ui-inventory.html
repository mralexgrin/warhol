Scout — UI Inventory

Every button, toggle/tab, and tag/badge class in the live app, pulled onto one page. Each specimen is the real production element (linked straight to scout-v52.css + alloy.css), so what you see here is what ships. Flip the theme to check both.

Why it feels all over the place

  • Two parallel button systems. Alloy's .btn* (utility) and Scout's .vbtn* (verbs) are both pill-shaped and similar in size — the split is intentional and documented, but at a glance they read as one family with inconsistent colour.
  • Four different "selected" languages. .seg marks selection with a white panel, .tsw with solid ink, .chipbtn with teal fill, and .vchip/.vgroup with a lilac→teal gradient. Four toggles, four visual grammars.
  • Six near-identical little labels. Prime consolidation target → .src-tag, .prelim-tag, .judged-tag, .proposedtag, and .pill are all 10px bold uppercase letter-spaced tags that differ mostly by colour. This is where the "sprawl" feeling really comes from.

Toggles & Tabs

Four selection patterns, each with its own visual grammar for "this one is active."

.seg button[aria-pressed]
Segmented control (Alloy). Selected = raised white panel.
.tsw button[aria-pressed]
Theme switch (Alloy). Selected = solid ink. Same job as .seg, different look.
.vchip .vgroup .vchip--joined .briefinfo .vchip--house .vchip--paused .vchip--new
Brief tabs. Selected tab = .vgroup wrapping the chip + a chevron disclosure, on a lilac→teal gradient. House = solid border, paused = dimmed, new = dashed.
