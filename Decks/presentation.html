The Problem

Thousands of creators have an audience and no business.

01

Small followings, obsessive engagement, deep trust. The audience is worth many times what they're extracting from it.

02

They're a one-person company doing sales, ops and invoicing at midnight. Most plateau there.

03

When the window closes they own nothing. No channel, no product, no asset. The value evaporates.

Why nobody finds them

Every discovery tool ranks by growth.

Which means they surface creators who are already winning — and already have agents, managers and three competing offers.

Ranking by momentum finds people thirty days early. We need to find them a year early.

The Signal

We look for a different shape.

Core

Monetization gap

High engagement, high trust, near-zero commercial infrastructure. The gap itself is the buy signal.

Timing

Operator strain

Cadence decay, abandoned side projects, publicly asking for help. Being one person has stopped working — this quarter.

Filter

Format fit

Clips well, repeatable, advertiser demand, adjacent to categories we already sell.

People who are great at making things and bad at running a business — which is exactly the business we're in.
