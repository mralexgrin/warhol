Find them before anyone is bidding.

Scout is an intelligence system for discovering emerging creators. It continuously monitors the open web, identifies creators gaining meaningful audience traction, and builds an evidence-backed picture of their potential. Scout turns a massive, constantly changing creator landscape into a focused pipeline of people worth knowing, evaluating, and backing.

Built this hackathon. The engine runs today on real creators; Scout is a working prototype you can open at the bottom of this page.

What it looks for

Four questions, asked of every creator. The first three are scored; Fit is a gate. A name only reaches the desk when the score clears a bar we measured rather than guessed.

Demand
Do people want to buy something from them?

Fit
Can we actually build something with them?
Gate · pass or fail
